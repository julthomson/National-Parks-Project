# National-Parks-Project

04/30/2026 - In the process of uploading scripts for replication. 

Data from this project were downloaded from the following open-access sources: 
- County-level voting data from MIT Election Data + Science Lab https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/VOQCHQ
- Geospatial data from the National Park Service (NPS). https://irma.nps.gov/DataStore/Reference/Profile/2224545?lnv=True
- Geospatial county data from the U.S. Census. https://www.census.gov/cgi-bin/geo/shapefiles/index.php 
- Population estimates from U.S. Department of Agriculture Economic Research Service https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/county-level-data-sets/county-level-data-sets-download-data
- Median income data from U.S. Department of Agriculture Economic Research Service https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/county-level-data-sets/county-level-data-sets-download-data
- Race/Ethnicity data from U.S. Census Bureau https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2023.B02001?t=Race+and+Ethnicity&g=010XX00US$0500000
- Education data from U.S. Census Bureau https://data.census.gov/table/ACSST5Y2023.S1501?t=Education&g=010XX00US$0500000
- Metropolitan/micropolitan data from U.S. Census https://catalog-beta.data.gov/dataset/tiger-line-shapefile-2018-nation-u-s-current-metropolitan-statistical-area-micropolitan-st
